# Ascended Punks Faction Rules
Welcome to Ascended Punks! We're a growing faction that helps newer players get stronger while we build up together. We alternate **war weeks** and **training weeks**. These rules keep things fair for everyone who shows up and puts in the work.

All times are in **TCT** (Torn City Time, same as UTC).

## Joining
- Join our Discord within **3 days** of joining the faction.
- Set your Discord nickname to your Torn name.
- Add your API key to TornStats (https://www.tornstats.com), YATA (https://yata.yt) and FFScouter (https://ffscouter.com). Make keys in your Torn API settings (https://www.torn.com/preferences.php#tab=api).
- Never share a Full Access key. Limited or Custom keys only. #Tools & API
- Read the rules and react ✅ to the rules message in #faction-edicts
## Trial period
- Your first **2 weeks** are a trial. Same rules as everyone else.
- Reach **level 15 within your first 2 weeks**. That's about a level a day, and attacking is the quickest way there. **Miss it, and depending on your progress, you'll get a strike or be removed.**
- At the end, management reviews your activity and confirms you as a Member.

# Activity & Absence
We don't expect you to play all day. We do expect you to tell us when you can't.

## Daily baseline
- Log in every day.
- Don't let your energy cap. Use it in the gym or on chains.
- Don't let your nerve cap. Use it on crimes.
-# This is a guideline, not a strike rule. It's how you grow.

## Going away
- Post in #leave-notices before you go: when you leave and when you'll be back.
- With a notice there's no time limit. Real life comes first.
- **3 days offline with no notice = 1 strike.**

## Around wars
Inactive members still count toward our war size, so this part is strict.
- **2+ days offline with no notice when we're enlisting for a war = removed.**
- **Offline during a war, or no hits at all in a war, without posting availability = 1 strike.**

# Organized Crimes & Chains

## Organized Crimes (OC)  #OC Guide
- Always be in an OC. When yours ends, join a new one within **24 hours**, in a role that fits your level.
- Only take roles where you meet the success minimum in the OC guide in #guides-hub.
- **Not in an OC within 24 hours while eligible and slots are open = warning.**
- **Still not in one 3 days after the warning = 1 strike.**
- Away with a notice in #leave-notices? Your OC time is excused.

## Chains
- Minimum **10 hits** per chain. Any hit counts, inside or outside a war.
- Under level 15: minimum **5 hits**. Chaining is also the quickest way to reach level 15, so jump in. #Getting to Level 15
- Stack your energy before big chains. Learn how: #Energy Stacking Guide  #Happy Jumping Guide
- Need outside targets? Try Target Finder (https://ffscouter.com/target-finder).
- Can't make it? Post in #war-availability  at least **24 hours** before the chain starts. Posting in time = fully exempt.
- **Missing the minimum without a notice = 1 strike.**
- Hit 10 and the Xanax you used is reimbursed. There's no cash payout for chains right now.

## Bonus hits
- Bonus hits land on 10, 25, 50, 100, 250, 500, 1,000 and up. #Chain guide
- Call it in #chain before you take one, and wait for the go-ahead.

# Ranked Wars: Before the War
We alternate war weeks and training weeks. On war weeks, everyone takes part.
Read the war guide in #guides-hub and the #Ranked war guide  #Chain guide

## Availability
- Leadership posts a poll in #war-availability  before each war. Answer it at least **24 hours** before the war starts.
- Then post your times: when you'll be on, and when you'll be offline. We use this to plan hosps and bounties for you.

## Prep
- Save your energy and stack for the start. New to stacking? #Energy Stacking Guide  #Happy Jumping Guide
- Set revives to **nobody** for the whole war.

## Scout the enemy
- Before the war starts, work out who you can and can't beat.
- Don't judge by level. Level holders stay low level on purpose and can have huge stats.
- Check estimated stats on FFScouter War Room (https://ffscouter.com/war-room), or install the FFScouter script (https://ffscouter.com/guides/ff-scouter-v2-installation) so stats show on every profile.
- Not sure? Ask in #war-room and someone will check for you.

# Ranked Wars: During the War

## Fighting
- Finish attacks with **Leave**. No mugging.
- Mugged an enemy by accident? Send the money back and say sorry, and you're fine. **Hiding a mug that management finds = 1 strike.**
- Call bonus hits in #war-room  first. **A bonus hit on an outside target during war = 1 strike.**
- Ask in #war-room  before dumping energy. It's usually fine if the chain is safe and you're not taking the bonus.
- Can't beat enemy targets? Make outside hits to keep our chain alive. Only hits that land in our chain count. We track it.
- **No hits at all in a war (war, chain or friendly hosp) without a notice = 1 strike.**

## Turtling: don't bleed points
Every hit an enemy lands on you scores for them, and hits abroad give them a 1.25x bonus.
- Going offline in Torn? Ask in #war-room  for a friendly hosp before you log off.
- Flying only protects you while you're in the air. Once you land, enemies can follow and farm you.
- Going offline abroad? Tell us in #war-room  first. We'll put a bounty on you so you get hospitalized there. The faction pays.
- Don't all fly to the same country.
- **Getting farmed while offline and not turtled (not in hospital or flying) = 1 strike.** Management's call, judged from the war report.
- Taking more hits than you make in **2 wars in a row** gets a warning DM with the turtling guide. **Do it again the next war = 1 strike.** One clean war resets it.

## Friendly hosps
- Hospitalizing a teammate to protect them counts like a war hit, for payout and for the 10-hit reimbursement. We track them.

## Termed wars
- If leadership agrees terms with the other faction, follow them exactly. **Breaking terms = removed.**

# Payouts, Vault & Armory

## War payouts
- Expenses (reimbursements and turtle bounties) come out first. The rest is split by hits.
- Every war hit counts. No minimum. Friendly hosps count as war hits.
- Outside hits during a war count only if they land in our chain. They earn a reduced share, set by leadership each war. Mugs, bounties and random hits don't count.

## Reimbursement
- Xanax used for wars and chains is reimbursed. Points refills, FHCs and energy cans aren't.
- You need **10 hits** in that war or chain to qualify. Same for every level. Friendly hosps count toward the 10.
- No claim needed. Leadership checks the logs.

## Faction vault
- War and OC payouts go into your faction vault balance.
- To withdraw, message the leader.
- Keep spare cash in the vault too. It can't be mugged there.

## Armory
- Return anything you borrowed and didn't use.

# Conduct

## In the faction
- Be respectful in Discord and faction chat.
- No scamming. Trades with members are always honest.
- Sparring with faction mates is fine if you both agree. No mugging each other.
- Got a problem with someone? Take it to management, not faction chat or #punk-plaza.

## Allies & other factions
- Don't hit our allies.
- In public (forums, global chat) you represent Ascended Punks. Don't start beef. Bring issues to management.

## Discord
- No NSFW.
- No spam or self-promo.
- No politics or religion.
- No harassment or slurs.

## Torn's rules
- Follow Torn's own rules. **Getting fedded or multi-accounting = removed.**

## Selling losses
- Not during wars or chains. Outside of them it's allowed, but we don't recommend it because it slows your growth.

# Strikes & Removal
We'd rather warn than kick. Strikes keep it fair for everyone.

## What gets a strike
- 3 days offline with no notice
- Offline during a war, or no hits at all in a war, without posting availability
- Missing the chain minimum without a notice
- Not in an OC 3 days after an OC warning (while eligible and slots are open)
- A bonus hit on an outside target during war
- Hiding a mug during a war (if management finds it)
- Getting farmed in a war while offline and not turtled
- Taking more hits than you make, war after war, after a warning
- Conduct breaks (disrespect, hitting allies, breaking Discord rules)

## How strikes work
- **Strike 1:** warning
- **Strike 2:** final warning
- **Strike 3:** management votes on removal
- Any member of management can issue a strike. You'll get a DM, and leadership logs it.
- Strikes clear after **60 days** with no new strikes.
- Think a strike was unfair? Appeal to the leader.

## Instant removal (no strikes)
- Scamming a member
- 2+ days offline with no notice when we're enlisting for a war
- Repeatedly hitting allies
- Leaking faction info or war plans
- Breaking Torn's rules (fedded, multi-accounting)
- Breaking agreed war terms

# Help, Roles & Leaving

## New players (under level 15)
- Ask anything in #ask-a-punk. Veterans and experienced members will jump in.
- Check #guides-hub for OC, war and general tips.
- Good first reads: #Getting to Level 15  · Battle stats (https://ffscouter.com/guides/battle-stats) · Fair fight explained (https://ffscouter.com/guides/fair-fight-explained)
- Your chain minimum is 5 hits instead of 10.

## Recruiters
Recruiters scout and invite players, post recruitment ads, onboard new members and keep an eye on trials. Want to help? Let management know.
- We welcome any level, as long as they've been active in the last 24 hours.

## Leaving
- Let management know before you go.
- Left on good terms? You're welcome back anytime.
- Removed? Rejoining is up to management.

## Leadership
- If the leader goes inactive, management decides the next step together.
