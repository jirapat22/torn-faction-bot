# Hospital (hosp)
Source: https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Hospital
Saved: 22/11/2026

## How to get out of hospital fast (all the ways out)
- Be revived by someone with the revive perk (see Getting out: revives).
- Use medical items: Small First Aid Kit, First Aid Kit, Morphine, Blood Bags. They take time off and add medical cooldown (max 6 hours, 9 with the faction upgrade).
- Opium: removes all standard hospital time, adds drug cooldown instead of medical cooldown.
- Neumune Tablet: the only item that works on hospital time from Radiation Poisoning.
- Early Discharge: after an outside hit during a war, leave with no items and no cooldown.
- Or wait it out.

## How you end up in hospital
- You start a fight and lose it, or the fight times out after 5 minutes.
- You are attacked and lose.
- You lose a game of Russian Roulette.
- You fail certain Crimes and Organized Crimes.
- You use Felovax while in jail.
- The casino's "spin the wheel" (outcomes like "face punch", "kick to the throat", "choke hold").
- Randomly, when drug addiction is over 9% while training at the gym without being under drug cooldown.
- Using Stink Bombs, Dog Poop or Toilet Paper on someone with much higher stats fails.
- Using a blood bag that doesn't match your blood type, Ipecac Syrup, or Blood Bag : Irradiated.
- Your faction is targeted by a Dirty Bomb, or you are a random victim of one.

## Getting out: revives
Being revived removes you from hospital and restores a percentage of your life based on the reviver's revive skill. It costs the reviver 75 energy, 5 less for each upgrade their faction has in the "Reviver" branch of the Fortitude tree (minimum 25). Love Juice during Valentine's Day cuts another 15 (minimum 10). To revive, you must first reach Brain Surgeon rank in the Medical starter job.

## Getting out: medical items and medical cooldown
Items that take time off hospital: Small First Aid Kit, First Aid Kit, Morphine, Blood Bags, Neumune Tablet, Opium.

Every one except Opium adds to your medical cooldown, a timer that grows each time you use a medical item. Over 6 hours, you can't use more medical items until it drops back under 6 hours. A faction upgrade can raise the cap to 9 hours. Medical items can be made up to 50% more effective: Education (+20%) and faction special upgrades (+30%).

Hospital time removed and life restored (standard / Education only / faction only / both):
- Small First Aid Kit (SFAK): 20 / 24 / 26 / 30 minutes, 5% / 6% / 6.5% / 7.5% life, +10 minutes medical cooldown.
- First Aid Kit (FAK): 40 / 48 / 52 / 60 minutes, 10% / 12% / 13% / 15% life, +15 minutes medical cooldown.
- Morphine: 70 / 84 / 91 / 105 minutes, 15% / 18% / 19.5% / 22.5% life, +20 minutes medical cooldown.
- Blood Bags: 120 / 144 / 156 / 180 minutes, 30% / 36% / 39% / 45% life, +30 minutes medical cooldown.

SFAKs, FAKs and Morphine can be stolen from the hospital in the Medical job, come in Boxes of Medical Supplies (THS stock benefit), or are sold at the Pharmacy.

## Blood bags
Fill an empty blood bag (needs the Intravenous Therapy education) to get one bag of your own blood type: filling it takes 30% of your life and adds 1 hour to your medical cooldown. Using a bag that doesn't match your blood type puts you in hospital instead.

## Opium
Opium (bought in China, Japan and South Africa, or from other players) removes all standard hospital time, restores 50% of your life and gives a 30% passive defense bonus. It adds no medical cooldown, but adds 120-180 minutes of drug cooldown.

## Neumune Tablet and radiation
The Neumune Tablet is the only medical item that removes hospital time caused by Radiation Poisoning (from a Dirty Bomb being built or set off, or an irradiated blood bag). It adds 3 hours of medical cooldown, takes 3 hours off hospital time and reduces Radiation Poisoning by 12 hours (1.5%). It does not remove hospital time for any other reason. Sold in Switzerland. A player with Radiation can't be revived.

## Early Discharge and outside hits
Early Discharge (since the Medical Revamp 2021) lets a member leave hospital with no medical items and no medical cooldown, after an "outside hit". It was added to balance faction warring. An alternative hospital icon shows when it is available, and your life goes back to what it was before the outside hitter attacked you.

- It is available straight away at the start of Ranked Wars and Territory Wars for all incoming outside hits.
- Since 11/11/2025 (Patch #408), it does not apply to faction members who are still recruits.
- Revive confirmations say if the patient has Early Discharge available, and using a medical item warns you if you have it.
- In the Elimination competition (September), all Elimination hits count as "inside hits", so they don't give Early Discharge.

## What you can and can't do in hospital
- You can use medical items, drugs and consumables, but not boosters, eggs or stat enhancers. In hospital for radiation poisoning, you can't use drugs.
- You can open parcels and supply packs, but empty boxes can't be made into new parcels.
- You can start, change, accept or cancel trades.
- You can't deposit into the faction (money or points).
- Hospital replaces jail: you can't be in both at once.
