# Revive
Source: https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Revive
Saved: 22/11/2026
Keywords: revive, revives, reviver, reviving, revive skill, revive chance, healer, brain surgeon

## What a revive does (how it works)
Reviving allows a player to get another player out of the hospital, no matter how long they are in the hospital for, or what their Medical cooldown is. A player can't be revived from hospital if they have Radiation. A reviver who is in the hospital themselves cannot revive anyone.

Revives are commonly used in faction wars, for hospitalisations from failed crimes, and for getting out of hospital abroad.

Since 23/09/2025 (Patch #402), any player inactive for 400 days or more can be revived (NPCs are not affected).

## How to revive someone (who can revive, becoming a reviver)
You gain the ability to revive once you reach the rank of Brain Surgeon in the Medical Job. You keep this ability even if you leave the job, and it is listed as a Perk on your homepage.

## Revive success chance
Revives have a success chance, shown to the reviver when attempting to revive a target. It depends on how many revives the target has recently received: at a base level each revive lowers the target's future success chance by around 8%, which slowly fades over 24 hours.

Before the Medical Revamp 2021, revives always succeeded and gave the target 25% of their maximum life.

## Revive skill
Revive skill goes from 1 to 100, based on the number of revives performed and the success chance of those revives (a successful revive at 10% chance gives 10x more skill). Revive skill has three effects:
1. It raises the base success chance from 90% up to 100%.
2. It reduces the effect of the target's previous revives on the success chance by up to 50%.
3. It sets the life restored by a revive: 80 revive skill = 80% life.

## Revive energy cost
A revive costs the reviver 75 energy, whether it succeeds or not. In a faction with the Fortitude branch set and Hospital Time upgraded to level 13, each reviving upgrade cuts the cost by 5 energy (minimum 25 energy per revive). Love Juice during Valentine's Day cuts it by another 15 energy.

## Revive settings
You choose who can revive you in your Preferences/Settings: 'Allow everyone', 'Allow friends and faction' (your friends list and your faction) or 'Nobody'. Trying to revive someone whose settings don't allow you gives the message 'This player does not want you to revive them'.

## Title and awards
With enough successful revives a player can sometimes take the title 'Healer', based on how highly they rank in revives compared to other players. There are several honor bars for reviving (Revive Awards).

Not everybody appreciates an unexpected revive.
