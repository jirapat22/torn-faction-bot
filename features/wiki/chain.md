## How chaining works (the 5 minute timer between hits)
A chain is a faction warring tool. It starts with a five minute "warm up" in which ten successful attacks must be made. Once a chain reaches ten, each next hit must land within a five minute timer: a successful hit resets the timer, adds one to the chain and keeps it going.

A successful attack can be made on anyone in Torn not in your own faction. After winning, any result counts (leave, mug, hospitalise). Only full members count towards a chain: someone who joins after the chain starts must wait out the 72 hour Recruit status.

## When a chain breaks (cooldown)
If no one in the faction makes a hit for 5 minutes, the chain breaks and cools down at 6 seconds per hit made, so a chain of 100 takes 600 seconds to reach zero. You can attack during the cooldown, but it has no effect on the cooling chain.

## Chain bonuses
The point of a chain is to reach the 13 bonus hits, each worth a big flat amount of respect. The Warlord weapon bonus does not apply to bonus hits, and no other multiplier does either.

- Hit 25: 20 respect, 150 seconds
- Hit 50: 40 respect, 5 minutes
- Hit 100: 80 respect, 10 minutes
- Hit 250: 160 respect, 25 minutes

To build longer chains, the faction unlocks each level by completing a challenge and paying a permanent respect cost. Upgrading mid-chain (challenge done, enough respect) does not break the chain.

## Respect per hit
Every attack a member makes on someone outside the faction earns respect, changed by these multipliers:
- Base respect: depends on the target's level, 1.00 at level 1 rising to 1.50 at level 100 (about level / 200 + 1).
- Chain scale: after hit 10, each hit's respect is multiplied by 0.25 x log10(hit number) + 0.75, slowly rising from 1x to 2x (hit 1,337 gives 1.53x). Not applied to bonus hits.
- Mugging: 0.75x. Every other result counts in full.
- Retaliation: 1.5x for a hospitalisation of someone who attacked one of your members in the last 5 minutes, once per player until they attack the faction again.
- Group attacks: 1.5 - 0.25 x 0.5^(x-1), where x is the number of extra attackers from your faction (3 extra = 1.4375x).
- War: 2x when hitting a faction you're at war with. In a territory war the opponent must be on their wall.
- Fair fight: 1x to 3x. Targets with about 75% of your battle stats or more give 3x, weaker targets tend towards 1x, and anyone above the 1000th highest battle stats gives the full 3x.
- Overseas: 1.25x when the target is in another country.

## Losing respect
Your faction loses 25% of the respect an opposing faction gains when one of their members attacks one of yours. At the higher bonuses this can be devastating.

## Chaining awards
- Chaining 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5: contribute to chains of 10, 100, 1,000, 10,000 and 100,000.
- Carnage, Massacre and Genocide: a single hit that gains the faction 10+, 100+ and 1000+ respect.
