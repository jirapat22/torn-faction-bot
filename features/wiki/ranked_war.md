# Ranked War
Source: https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Ranked_War
Saved: 22/11/2026

## What a ranked war is
Ranked warring is a warring mode where factions fight one another 1v1 to move up through ranked tiers and earn rewards. It is a "tug of war": each side attacks members of the other to gain score, and the first to cross the target net score wins.

## Enlisting
The leader, co-leader or anyone with the "War Management" permission can enlist the faction from the "Ranked" tab on the faction page, and enlist or withdraw at any time.
- The faction needs at least 10 full members (recruits included) active in the last 24 hours.
- Recruits can still be hit during the war, so they give the opponent score.
- A faction can't take part if its roster strength has dropped a lot since its last war while its rank is higher than expected.
- Members in Federal Jail count towards size; the faction is kicked out of matchmaking if more than 10% of it is fedded. Fallen members don't count.
- The check that locks or unlocks enlisting runs once an hour.

## Matchmaking
All matchmaking happens at 12:00 TCT on Tuesdays: every enlisted faction goes into one pool and the system pairs them. It matches on:
- Faction Rating (hidden): stat strength (from members' battle stats) plus performance (an Elo-style score updated after every ranked war).
- Faction size: opponents between round((members / 1.25) - 5) and round((members + 5) x 1.25) members.
- Recently fought: at least 6 wars must pass before the same two factions meet again.

Members with extremely high stats have capped scores for matchmaking. Recruits are counted.

Factions can prefer weekday (Thursday or Friday) or weekend (Saturday) wars. If both sides want the same, they get it; otherwise it's random. The day is then picked at random within that choice, and the start time is the most active period, on average, of both factions' members on that day in the past. Everyone gets an event with the start time and opponent, also shown on the Ranked tab.

Sacrifice matches: a very strong faction with no fair opponent is sometimes given one it outclasses. The aim is that a faction is on the receiving end less than 5% of the time, with a long protection period after. Unmatched weeks happen by design, and an unmatched faction goes to the front of the queue the following weeks.

## War templates
Since 03/11/2026, a weekly Template can change one thing about that week's ranked wars for every enlisted faction: either the War Mode (what you chase and how the war ends) or one Condition (e.g. what a hit is worth). The Enlistment Panel shows next week's template once the previous enlistment closes. Known templates: 50% reduced score requirement; Day War (if no one reaches the target in 24 hours, the higher score wins).

## Ranks and divisions
Five ranks, each with four divisions: Bronze (about 30% of ranked factions), Silver (25%), Gold (20%), Platinum (15%), Diamond (10%). Rank and division come from your faction rating position among all factions, re-checked after every war. Diamond needs at least 75 members. Diamond factions also hold a numbered position: beating a faction with a better position takes it, and the loser drops one.

Rank decay: a faction that doesn't fight a ranked war for about six weeks is unranked outright, with a warning a week before. Staying enlisted prevents this. Re-enlisting gives a new rank at matchmaking.

## During the war
- Score = the respect earned by each hit on the opponent, so mugs give 75% of a leave or hosp, and chain bonus hits give a lot.
- Any active chain is forced into cooldown 5 seconds before the war starts.
- Early Discharge is available from the start for all incoming outside hits.
- Members of both factions get 60 seconds of protection from outside hitters after leaving hospital or jail.
- Nobody can join, leave or be kicked from either faction until the war ends.

## Target score, how long a war lasts and how it ends
The target net score depends on both factions' ranks and member counts. If it isn't met within 24 hours, it drops by 1% of the original value every hour, so a war can last at most 123 hours. There is normally no forfeit: a war ends only when one side reaches the target or a faction is destroyed. If a faction loses 20% or more of its members to federal jail, the other side can file a bug report and admins can force a forfeit.

## Rewards
Participants get bonus respect and Caches with special weapons and armour. What changes the size:
- Rank and division: higher pays more, and each division up adds more.
- Outcome: a win is 2x, a loss 1x.
- Member count: +1% for each full member over 10 (factor 1 + (members - 10) / 100).
- Participation: 0x to 3x, win or lose.
- Random variance: +10% to -10%.
